01 — Who we are

One partner for the life of your facility.

NAJMARA's business is built around the delivery, care and supply needs of critical sites, buildings and facilities across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. Four complementary pillars bring these together under one accountable partner.

Data Centre Projects — our main focus — covers the installation, testing and commissioning of the power, cooling, fire and control systems that keep data centres live, followed by their operation and maintenance and the fibre networks that connect them.
