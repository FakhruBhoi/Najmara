Who we are

One partner for the life of your facility.

NAJMARA brings the delivery, care and supply needs of critical sites, buildings and facilities across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia under a single accountable partner.

01 — What drives us

Reliable work,
lasting relationships.

Four complementary pillars — data centre projects, maintenance & facility management, general contracting and equipment supply — work to one standard: planned work, documented reporting and a named person accountable for every contract.

Vision

To be the partner of choice.

To be the partner of choice for data centre projects, integrated maintenance and facility management, general contracting and equipment supply in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, recognised for reliability, quality workmanship and long-term relationships.

Mission

Keep facilities running at their best.

To keep facilities running at their best and deliver dependable construction and supply solutions, by combining skilled people, disciplined planning and the right materials for every project, with a focus on quality, safety and long-term value.

02 — Values

Quality. Safety.
Integrity. Responsiveness.

Four standards applied to every task, every site and every stage of the contract — big or small.

03 — Our approach

How we deliver

One point of accountability, from planning to handover — the same five steps whether the contract is a single data hall or a full facility.

01

Scope agreed in writing

Services, frequencies and responsibilities are set out before work starts.

02

A named contact

One accountable person for every contract, who knows the site, team and scope.

03

Planned, not reactive

Routine work is scheduled in advance and tracked against the plan.

04

Qualified teams

Supervisors and technicians assigned to the scope they are trained for.

05

Reported and handed over

Agreed reporting cycles; works hand over with testing records and follow-up.

04 — What we do

Four pillars,
one accountable partner.

Data Centre Projects installs, tests and commissions the power, cooling, fire and control systems that keep data centres live, then operates and maintains them and the fibre that connects them. Maintenance & Facility Management keeps buildings clean, safe and running. General Contracting delivers civil, MEP and finishing works through to handover. Equipment & Material Supply sources the electrical, building and specialist materials our clients' projects need.
